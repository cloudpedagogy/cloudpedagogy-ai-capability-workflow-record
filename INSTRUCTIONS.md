# AI Capability Workflow Record — User Instructions

## What the tool does
CloudPedagogy AI Capability Workflow Record supports both AI capability development and lightweight governance through structured human-AI workflow documentation.

## How capability is developed through use
Capability is developed by guiding users through structured workflows where they must parameterize AI prompts, critically evaluate AI outputs, and record refined human judgments. This process builds practical experience in co-agency with AI.

## How governance is supported
Governance is supported through visibility. By documenting the human decisions made at each step and providing space for rationale, risks, and assumptions, the tool creates a reviewable audit trail of AI-assisted work.

## Explanation of optional fields
The **Capability & Governance Notes** section at the bottom of the runner includes:
- **Capability Alignment**: Optional notes on what capability this workflow supports, suggested AI use patterns, or reflection prompts.
- **Governance & Transparency**: Optional notes on AI involvement, assumptions, risks, concerns, or rationale for specific decisions.

These fields are non-blocking and designed to support professional judgment rather than constrain it.
